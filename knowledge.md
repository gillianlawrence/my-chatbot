# BiteBuddy knowledge notes
Source: Marker 7 Coastal Grill dinner menu (dated 08.08.23). Prices may have changed.

## Cheapest Starter at Marker 7
The cheapest starter is the Soup of the day, at $6 for the smaller size and $10 for the larger. The next cheapest starter is Fried Green Tomatoes at $10. Most other starters cost $11-$15.

## Number of Oysters in the Oyster Dishes
Oysters Rockefeller comes with 6 oysters, baked with a spinach and parmesan cream sauce and topped with parmesan bread crumbs. Oysters Bienville also comes with 6 oysters, baked with a shrimp and mushroom cream sauce and topped with parmesan bread crumbs. Raw oysters are a seasonal selection served with cocktail sauce, horseradish, and crackers. The menu doesn't list a count for them, and all oysters are priced at Market Price.

## Price of the Crab Cakes
The Crab Cakes cost $26. The order is 2 seared Blue Claw crab cakes topped with remoulade, with a choice of two sides.

## Number of Salads Offered
Marker 7 offers 4 salads: House Salad, Caesar Salad, Spinach Salad, and Wedge Salad. Salads cost $7 for a small and $10 for a large, but the Wedge Salad is only available in large. You can add chicken for $6, shrimp for $7, or salmon or tuna for $8.

## Platter Deal
You choose 2 platter items for $21. Each platter comes with hushpuppies and your choice of 2 sides. The platter options are Shrimp, Oysters, Grouper, Clam Strips, Calamari, and Artichoke Hearts.

## Side Dish Prices and Options
Sides cost $4 each or 3 for $11. The options are Black Beans & Rice, Hushpuppies, Baked Smoked Gouda Macaroni & Cheese, Red Mule Organic Grits (add cheese for $.50), Steamed Broccoli, Roasted Sweet Potatoes, French Fries, Coleslaw, Green Beans with Peppers & Onions, and Grilled Asparagus.

## Most Expensive Entree
The Surf & Turf is the priciest listed entree at $38. It comes with a grilled 10 oz Ribeye and your choice of shrimp or scallops, served with grilled asparagus and roasted sweet potatoes. The Fresh Catch is priced at Market, so it has no fixed price on the menu.

## Location, Parking, and Gratuity
Marker 7 Coastal Grill is at 1195 South Milledge Ave. in Athens, GA, and the phone number is 706.850.3451. Parking is available across the street at Fire Station 3. An 18% gratuity is added to parties of 6 or more.
